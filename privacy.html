Longrunner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Summary

Longrunner works inside your browser. We do not collect, sell or share your browsing data. We have no servers that store your saved ads.

What the extension reads

Longrunner runs only on Meta Ad Library pages (facebook.com/ads/library). It reads the ads shown on the page you are viewing (library ID, advertiser name, ad text, start date, media links) to show how long each ad has been running and to let you download or save it. It does not run on any other website and does not read your Facebook account, messages or profile.

Where your data is stored

Saved ads, folders, tags, notes, tracked brands and settings are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage). They never leave your device. Uninstalling the extension deletes them.

Payments

Pro subscriptions are processed by ExtensionPay and Stripe. If you upgrade, they receive your email address and payment details to manage your subscription. We receive your email and subscription status only. See ExtensionPay's privacy policy and Stripe's privacy policy.

Configuration updates

The extension periodically downloads a small configuration file from our website so it keeps working when the Ad Library page changes. This request contains no personal data.

Downloads and content

Downloaded ads are saved to your computer for your personal research. Ad creatives belong to their owners. Longrunner is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc.
