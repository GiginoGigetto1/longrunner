Longrunner – Terms & Refunds

Last updated: October 20, 2026

The service

Longrunner is a Chrome extension that helps you research ads in the Meta Ad Library. The free plan is free forever. Longrunner Pro is a subscription at $9.99/month or $59.99/year that unlocks advanced features.

Billing

Payments are processed by ExtensionPay and Stripe. Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period until you cancel. Prices may include taxes where required.

Cancellation

You can cancel anytime from the extension ("Restore purchase" / account page) or by emailing us. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds

If Longrunner Pro is not for you, email us within 7 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full. Renewals are not refunded, but you can cancel before the next renewal.

Acceptable use

Downloaded ads are for your personal research. Ad creatives belong to their owners: do not republish them as your own. Longrunner only works on pages you open yourself and does not collect data in the background.

No affiliation

Longrunner is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc. Meta and Facebook are trademarks of Meta Platforms, Inc.

Warranty

Longrunner depends on the Meta Ad Library, which can change without notice. We fix issues as fast as we can, but the service is provided "as is", without guarantees of uninterrupted availability.
